The calm default

The home view is deliberately under-populated. It renders a single next action, an optional energy check-in, and a collapsed module list — the minimum needed to begin work.

Research basis

  • Morris, Begel and Wiedermann (2015) found that neurodiverse knowledge workers are blocked less by task difficulty than by ambiguous, cluttered interfaces.
  • Sweller (1988): removing extraneous elements protects working memory for the task itself.
  • Spiel et al. (2022) warn against deficit framing — the screen offers control, not correction.

Norman's principles

Visibility

The next physical action is the largest element on screen; nothing else is above it.

Constraints

One start button. Secondary modules sit below the fold and collapse entirely in quiet mode.

Feedback

The check-in chip confirms the choice inline; the layout changes immediately.

Interaction notes
