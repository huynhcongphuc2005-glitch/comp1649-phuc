A place to stop

Overload mode removes work entirely. It offers one paced breathing exercise, three ways to reduce stimulation, and direct routes to human support.

Research basis

  • Van Hees, Moyson and Roeyers (2015) report that autistic students' support needs are frequently social and emotional, not only academic.
  • McDowall and Kiseleva (2024) argue that isolated apps are weak substitutes for integrated institutional support.
  • Zervogianni et al. (2020): reliability and engagement depend on the user being able to trust and stop the system.

Norman's principles

Constraints

No task list, no deadlines, no "resume" pressure. The only forward actions are breathing or leaving.

Feedback

The orb changes size and the phase label names the state; the countdown is visible without being urgent.

Affordance

Support contacts are plain tappable rows with explicit labels, not buried in a help centre.

Interaction notes

The breathing pacer runs a 4-4-4-4 cycle. The first switch applies the night palette to the whole artboard; the others are local preference states.
