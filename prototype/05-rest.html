Closing the loop

Completion is acknowledged in one sentence, with no confetti, no score, and no streak that can be broken. The next step is previewed but explicitly deferred.

Research basis

  • Zervogianni et al. (2020) identify engagement as one of three evidence categories the autism community expects from digital support — measured by continued, voluntary use rather than compulsion.
  • Spiel et al. (2022): reward mechanics that punish rest reinforce neuro-normative productivity norms.
  • Sweller (1988): a single summary removes the need to reconstruct what was achieved.

Norman's principles

Feedback

The tick, the confirmation sentence and the progress meter all confirm the same event, immediately.

Constraints

Only two exits are offered: take a break, or return to Today. There is no "keep going" pressure.

Visibility

The completed step and the next step are shown together so the plan still makes sense.

Interaction notes

Start runs a three-minute rest countdown in place. Back to Today returns to screen 01 with the plan advanced.
