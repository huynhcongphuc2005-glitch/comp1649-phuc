Screen 09 / 09 · Support

Progress without pressure

Progress is summarised in plain counts, including rest days. The screen then routes outward, to people, because software is not a substitute for support.

Research basis

  • McDowall and Kiseleva (2024) reviewed 11 reviews of higher-education support and found technology-based interventions to be only one modest strand of effective provision.
  • Van Hees, Moyson and Roeyers (2015): social and emotional support predicts adjustment more strongly than academic tools alone.
  • Spiel et al. (2022): measuring compliance with neurotypical productivity norms misrepresents success.

Norman's principles

Feedback

Time, steps and rest are reported separately, so no single number implies judgement.

Affordance

Support is presented as three tappable rows with named consequences, not an external link dump.

Consistency

The visual grammar of cards, rows and chevrons matches the rest of the product.

Interaction notes

Bars are descriptive only; rest days use the neutral surface token rather than an error colour. Sharing requires an explicit, scoped consent step described in the study materials.
