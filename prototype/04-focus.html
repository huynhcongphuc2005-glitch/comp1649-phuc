Time you can see

Duration is shown as a closing ring and a whole number of minutes. Seconds are removed by default because a ticking figure competes for attention and raises time pressure.

Research basis

  • Cuber et al. (2024) found that a bounded, low-distraction study environment improved concentration and motivation for university students with ADHD.
  • Sonne, Obel and Grønbæk (2015) argue that real-time assistance should sense and support attention without adding demand.
  • Zervogianni et al. (2020) place engagement and reliability alongside effectiveness — a timer nobody trusts is not usable.

Norman's principles

Feedback

The ring updates continuously but silently; the caption states the state in words, not just colour.

Visibility

Remaining time and current task are both permanently on screen; nothing is hidden behind a menu.

Consistency

Start, pause and reset keep identical positions and labels across every timer state.

Interaction notes

Press Start focus to run the 15-minute countdown; the ring contracts in real time. Reset restores 15. The check icon ends the step early and moves to screen 05.
