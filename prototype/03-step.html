Removing ambiguity

The detail view answers the three questions that stall task initiation: what exactly do I do, how will I know it is done, and how small can it get?

Research basis

  • Morris, Begel and Wiedermann (2015): unclear requirements are a primary barrier reported by neurodiverse employees, more than technical difficulty.
  • Teevan, Iqbal and von Veh (2016): micro-tasks work best when the completion state is unambiguous.
  • Spiel et al. (2022): the user keeps authority — the split is offered, never forced.

Norman's principles

Affordance

Checkboxes look tickable; the search strings expose a visible Copy action rather than hidden long-press.

Visibility

The completion criteria sit above the search tools, so the goal is visible before the method.

Constraints

"No writing needed" explicitly rules out the behaviour that makes this step feel larger than it is.

Interaction notes

Ticking all three outcomes prepares the completion state. Split rewrites the duration to 5 minutes and updates the primary button label — a live demonstration of a self-imposed constraint.
