Evidence over gimmick

Typography is adjustable, and the default choice follows the strongest available evidence rather than the most marketed product.

Research basis

  • Rello and Baeza-Yates (2013) found that typeface, size and spacing measurably affect reading speed and comprehension for dyslexic readers.
  • Wery and Diliberto (2017) found no reading-rate or accuracy benefit from OpenDyslexic in a controlled alternating-treatment design.
  • The design conclusion is therefore: default to legibility and user control, not to a novelty font.

Norman's principles

Feedback

Every slider updates the preview instantly, so the effect is visible before it is saved.

Affordance

Consistency
