Screen 08 / 09 · Sensory

Personalisation, not prescription

A single settings surface exposes colour, motion and sound. Every control states its effect in plain language and applies immediately.

Research basis

  • Morris, Begel and Wiedermann (2015) found that individualisation of environment was one of the most valued accommodations reported by neurodiverse workers.
  • Zervogianni et al. (2020) argue that digital support should be co-developed with its community and transparent about what it changes.
  • Van Hees, Moyson and Roeyers (2015) highlight sensory and emotional regulation as core support needs in higher education.

Norman's principles

Visibility

Every option is on one screen with a visible swatch, name and consequence — no sub-menus.

Affordance

Full-width rows and iOS-style switches afford tapping; selected states use a tick, not colour alone.

Consistency

The same tokens drive every screen, so a change here is never partial.

Interaction notes

Selecting Paper, Warm or Night re-themes the entire artboard live. Switches record a local preference only, and the reduction is visible in the very next transition. Reading & type size continues to screen 07.
